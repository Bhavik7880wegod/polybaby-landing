Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 26, 2026

PolyBaby is a Polymarket intelligence service. This page explains what data we collect (almost none) and how we handle it.

What we collect on this website

This landing page does not collect personal information. We do not use cookies, trackers, analytics, or fingerprinting on this page. No login, no email capture, no forms.

If you join the Discord

Joining the PolyBaby Discord server means your interaction is governed by Discord's privacy policy and terms of service. You can review them at discord.com/privacy. PolyBaby cannot access information that Discord does not surface to server operators (your Discord username and any messages you post in public channels).

What PolyBaby publishes inside Discord

PolyBaby posts publicly observable Polymarket trade data — wallet addresses, trade amounts, market prices — sourced directly from on-chain Polygon transactions and Polymarket's public APIs. No private data is involved.

Third parties

Outbound links from this page (Discord, email) are governed by those services' own privacy policies. PolyBaby is not affiliated with Polymarket, Discord, or any prediction market operator.

Disclaimer

PolyBaby is for informational and entertainment purposes only. Nothing posted in the Discord constitutes financial advice. Prediction markets involve financial risk. Verify your local laws before participating.
