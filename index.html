The Polymarket tracker built by traders

PolyBaby is a real-time Polymarket tracker that follows the top 50 traders on the platform and surfaces high-conviction calls in Discord 30 minutes before sports events tip off. Every signal is sourced from on-chain Polymarket activity — whale positions, fresh-wallet sweeps, top-trader follows, smart-money divergence — and posted to a public Discord with a verifiable track record.

If you're searching for a Polymarket whale tracker, a way to follow top Polymarket traders, or a feed that catches when smart money diverges from the public, this is what PolyBaby does — across every active sports market on Polymarket, in real time.

What PolyBaby tracks

  • Top-trader alerts — fires whenever a top-50 Polymarket trader (by lifetime PnL) takes a fresh position
  • Whale alerts — fires on positions over $10K from any wallet
  • Fresh-wallet swarms — multiple new wallets stacking the same side within minutes (often the earliest insider tell)
  • Smart-money divergence — gap between public market price and what informed wallets are buying, only fires above 15%
  • Pre-game sports picks — 30-minute pre-tipoff signals across NBA, NFL, NHL, MLB, soccer (Premier League, La Liga, Champions League), tennis, golf, F1, UFC, esports, IPL cricket

Verifiable track record

Every PolyBaby call is logged with the exact Polymarket URL, entry price, side, and timestamp before the event resolves. The cumulative track record is shown live in the dashboard above: — wins / — losses / —% accuracy across — calls. Every win is independently verifiable on Polymarket itself — no deletion, no curated highlight reels.
